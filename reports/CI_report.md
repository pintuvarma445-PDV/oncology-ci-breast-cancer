## 1. Global Epidemiology Overview

### 1.1 Global Burden  
- Breast cancer is the **most common cancer globally**.  
- Highest incidence seen in **Western Europe, North America, Australia** due to widespread screening.  
- Mortality remains high in **Asia and Africa**, driven by late diagnosis and limited access to care.  

### 1.2 India Burden  
- India shows rapidly rising incidence with significant regional variations:  
  - Highest rates in **Delhi, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad**  
- Higher mortality-to-incidence ratio highlights late-stage detection challenges.

## 3. Intervention Trends

Top therapeutic mechanisms identified through text mining include:

- **HER2-targeted therapies**  
- **CDK4/6 inhibitors**  
- **Immunotherapy (PD-1/PD-L1)**  
- **Antibody–Drug Conjugates (ADCs)**  
- **Hormonal/endocrine therapy**  

These reflect a global shift toward **precision oncology and targeted biologics**.

## 5. Strategic CI Insights for Pharma

### 5.1 Market Opportunity  
mBC reflects significant unmet needs in:  
- Endocrine-resistant disease  
- Rapid metastatic progression  
- Limited durable response in heavily pre-treated patients  

### 5.2 Portfolio Differentiation  
- HER2 and HR+ segments are **highly competitive**  
- Clear gaps persist in **Triple-Negative Breast Cancer (TNBC)**  
- ADCs and Immunotherapy combinations show strong future potential  

### 5.3 Competitive Signals  
- Early-stage innovation surge → opportunity for **partnerships and acquisitions**  
- Differentiation must focus on:  
  - Novel biomarkers  
  - Combination regimens  
  - Patient subtyping
